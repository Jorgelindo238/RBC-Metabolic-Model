# Agentic FLA Vmax-only pilot

User-authorized departure from repeated one-factor probes. Three parallel roles:
executed ATP-demand attribution, pinned RBC-GEM structural curation, and bounded
Vmax search. The controller freezes source inputs and numerical acceptance;
agents cannot promote their own hypotheses. Deterministic calculation and
original-rate verification adjudicate candidates. This is an agent-orchestrated
research workflow, not a claim of an autonomous production service.

GLUT law, availability threshold, OPLAH, source values and zero capacities.

containing17 positive Vmax parameters. Common AK/NDPK factors preserve the

PGM/ENO are a computationally grouped throughput test, not a claim of a shared
enzyme. Factor boxes are engineering exploration bounds; HK retains its hard
5mM/day ceiling. Record all boundary hits and correlated/flat outcomes.

## Search and adjudication

First search actual J4 only, on the onset of the energy failure; J8/J11/J15
are not used by the optimizer. J1 is initialization, not an error term.
J1/J4 alone set absolute working scales; the relative part uses the relevant
observation, not a fitted assay variance. Derived charge, total and executed
budgets are diagnostics, not extra independent likelihood terms. ATP/ADP/AMP
log-error and nonadenylate working MSE are separate objectives. B13PG/P2G
aliases stay audit-only; B23PG/P3G nominal scores are guards only, because
their physical identities are unverified. Zero-scale observations retain a
separate numerical absolute guard without an invented assay detection limit.

Report a bounded Pareto search, baseline and explored trade-offs even if no
improved point passes. Protect per-pool canonical MSE and individual/timewise
adenylates using the previous1% engineering rules against the same native
reference. The candidate must improve mean log-adenylate error by at least0.1%
and pass all guards. Choose a bounded shortlist before looking at later data.
If none is admissible, replay a named diagnostic trade-off and keep its
violations visible. No silent relaxation or identification of a global best.

Reintegrate selected points to J15 with strict/ultrastrict settings, compare
actual J4 and J8/J11/J15 separately, and verify reaction-consistent executed
balances, positivity and independent native-rate projection. Later values
are historically exposed, not blind or donor-independent validation. Do not
promote to default or claim physiological recovery from relative error alone.
